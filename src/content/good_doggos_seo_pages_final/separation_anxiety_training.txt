Building Independence through Structure
At Good Doggos, I don't just "manage" the anxiety until you return. For 8 years, I’ve specialized in helping Riverside County dogs overcome the desperate need to be attached to their owners. My balanced approach focuses on building a foundation of impulse control. I use rewards for crate success, but I also implement clear boundaries.

I once worked with a dog that had chewed through drywall in a Hemet home. We used "Place" training and my helper dog Simba to model calm independence. While the owner was "pretend" leaving—practicing with keys and shoes—we used Simba to show the dog that "distance" doesn't mean "abandonment." By systematically building the dog's ability to self-regulate while the owner was in another room, we finally broke the panic cycle.
