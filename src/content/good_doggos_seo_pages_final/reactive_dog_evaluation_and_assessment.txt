Getting Under the Hood of the Behavior
At Good Doggos, my evaluation isn't a sales pitch—it’s a deep dive into your dog’s psychology. For 8 years, I’ve been the go-to for Riverside County owners with dogs that "positive-only" shops won't touch. I look at the "why." Is it genuine aggression or a lack of impulse control? Are they terrified or just entitled?

I once assessed a dog labeled "aggressive" near Hiram Deville Park. The evaluation revealed it wasn't aggression at all, but rather "frustrated greeting" mixed with poor leash mechanics. The dog was simply desperate to get to other dogs and had learned that screaming worked. By diagnosing the root cause, we created a balanced roadmap to neutrality that the owner could actually execute. We stopped the "guessing game" and started training.
