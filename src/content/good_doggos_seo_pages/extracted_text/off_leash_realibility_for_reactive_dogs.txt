The idea of letting a reactive dog off-leash sounds like a nightmare to most owners. You’ve likely spent years death-gripping a six-foot lead, bracing your core every time another dog appears on the horizon at Hiram Deville Park. You want that dream of watching your dog run free—maybe at the base of the San Jacinto Mountains or in a quiet field—but the "what ifs" are paralyzing. What if they bolt? What if they lunge at a hiker? Most trainers will tell you that a reactive dog can never be trusted off-leash. We think that’s a cop-out. You don't need to keep your dog in a permanent "leash-jail"; you need a level of communication that doesn't rely on a piece of nylon.
Beyond the Physical Tether
At Good Doggos, we specialize in the high-level communication required to take a reactive dog off-leash safely. For 8 years, we’ve been proving that reactivity doesn't have to mean a lifetime of restriction. Our balanced approach uses E-collar technology not as a "punishment," but as a silent, long-distance "tap" on the shoulder. We use high-value rewards and heavy engagement to make you the center of your dog’s world, even when the distractions of Riverside County are swirling around them. But we also provide a fair, calibrated correction for when a reactive dog decides to "fixate" or ignore a recall. Without a way to enforce the command at a distance, you aren't training—you’re just gambling.
