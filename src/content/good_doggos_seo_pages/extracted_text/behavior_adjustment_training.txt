Functional Rewards and Real-World Control
At Good Doggos, we take a more sophisticated approach to reactivity. We use Behavior Adjustment Training principles, but we do it with the backbone of a balanced system. The goal is to teach your dog that they have choices other than "fight or flight." We’ve spent 8 years in Riverside County showing owners how to read the subtle body language that happens before the explosion. We use high-value rewards and "functional" rewards—like giving the dog the distance they crave—to reinforce calm behavior. However, we also believe in clear boundaries. If your dog decides to ignore your guidance and launch a lunging fit, we use calibrated leash corrections to provide a clear "no," bringing their brain back to a place where they can actually learn.
