The Balanced Fix for the "Friendly Frustrated" and the Fearful
Leash reactivity is rarely about "hating" other dogs. Usually, it’s a mix of pent-up frustration and a total lack of direction from the person holding the leash. At Good Doggos, we specialize in the Leash Reactivity Bootcamp, where we strip away the noise and get back to basics. We use a balanced approach because a reactive dog is a dog in an emotional red-zone. We use high-value rewards to build a positive association with triggers, but we also introduce clear, fair leash communication. If your dog is throwing a tantrum, they need to know that behavior is unacceptable—not just that a treat is waiting if they stop.
We don't just train in a sterile facility. We get out into the San Jacinto sun and work in the environments where you actually live. We teach you how to read your dog's body language before the "explosion" happens. Most owners miss the subtle stare or the stiffening of the tail that happens seconds before the barking starts. By the time we’re done, you’ll have the confidence to walk past the barking dogs behind the fences on Esplanade Ave without breaking a sweat.
