You might be afraid to walk past your dog while they’re chewing a bone, or maybe you’ve started "bribing" them with treats just to get your own shoe back. Let’s be clear: trading a steak for a stolen item isn't training; it’s a ransom payment. In a Riverside County household with kids or guests, you cannot afford to have a dog that thinks they "own" the living room.
Reclaiming Your Space and Your Gear
