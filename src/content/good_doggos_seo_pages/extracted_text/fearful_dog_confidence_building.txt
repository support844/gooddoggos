If your dog spends most of their life trying to disappear into the floorboards, you know how heartbreaking it is. Maybe they’re spooked by the loud garbage trucks on San Jacinto Avenue, or perhaps they refuse to walk past a neighbor’s lawn decorations on a windy Riverside County afternoon. You’ve probably tried to "comfort" them out of it, but soothing a terrified dog often just validates their suspicion that the world is indeed a scary place. It’s exhausting to watch your dog live in a state of constant "environmental lock," where a simple trip to Hiram Deville Park feels like a walk through a haunted house.
Building a Foundation of Certainty
At Good Doggos, we don’t just "pity" fearful dogs; we empower them. For the last 8 years, we’ve specialized in taking dogs from a state of shut-down anxiety to a state of curious engagement. Our balanced approach is the key here. While we use heavy doses of praise and high-value rewards to mark "brave" choices, we also provide the physical structure a nervous dog craves. A fearful dog is a dog without a leader; they think they have to monitor every threat themselves. By using calibrated leash communication, we show them that we are handling the environment so they don't have to.
