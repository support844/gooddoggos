Rewiring the Emotional Brain
At Good Doggos, we don't just mask the symptoms; we change how your dog actually feels about their triggers. Desensitization is about exposure at a level your dog can handle—starting at a distance where they see the "scary thing" but don't blow up. Counter-conditioning is the "magic" part where we pair that trigger with something high-value. But here is where we differ from the "purely positive" crowd: we use a balanced approach. While we use plenty of praise and rewards to build a new, happy association, we also maintain clear boundaries. If a dog in Riverside County tries to escalate into a dangerous lunging fit, we use calibrated leash corrections to provide the structure they need to stay under threshold and actually learn.
