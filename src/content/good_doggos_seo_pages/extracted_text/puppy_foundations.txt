Building Success from Day One
At Good Doggos, we don’t believe puppies are too young for clear communication. For the last 8 years, we’ve shown Riverside County owners that "balanced" training for puppies is about setting the stage for a lifetime of neutrality. We use heavy doses of food rewards, toys, and verbal praise to build a massive "bank account" of engagement—making you the most exciting thing in their world. But we also introduce the concept of boundaries early. We use gentle leash fundamentals and age-appropriate structure to teach your puppy that "no" exists. If you only ever teach them "yes," they’ll have a total meltdown the first time life tells them they can't do something.
