Breaking the Reactive Cycle
At Good Doggos, we specialize in the dogs that other trainers label "unfixable." Our balanced approach isn't about suppressing your dog; it's about shifting their focus. We use high-value rewards and enthusiastic praise to reward the choice to remain calm, but we also use calibrated leash corrections to interrupt the "loading" phase—that moment where your dog stares, stiffens, and prepares to explode. If you don't stop the mental fixating, you’ll never stop the physical lunging.
