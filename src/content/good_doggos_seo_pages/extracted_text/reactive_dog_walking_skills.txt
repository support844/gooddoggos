Mastering the Art of Neutrality
At Good Doggos, we don't just teach your dog to "heel"—we teach you how to navigate the world with a reactive animal. For 8 years, we’ve specialized in the high-stakes walking skills that "purely positive" trainers often avoid. Our balanced approach is built on the foundation of neutrality. We use high-value rewards and heavy praise to reinforce the moments your dog chooses you over the environment. But we also provide a fair, physical "interrupt" using calibrated leash corrections. If you don't have a way to tell your dog "no" the moment they begin to fixate on a trigger, you’re just waiting for the next explosion.
