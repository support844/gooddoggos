Cooperative Care and Calm Handling
At Good Doggos, we specialize in the "hard" veterinary cases—the dogs that vets have labeled "aggressive" or "difficult." For 8 years, we’ve been helping Riverside County owners turn a traumatic event into a neutral one. Our balanced approach focuses on "Cooperative Care." We use high-value rewards and heavy praise to reward your dog for holding still and accepting touch. However, we also believe in the power of clear, physical boundaries. We use calibrated leash pressure and structured handling drills to show your dog that "opting out" through biting or thrashing isn't an option. If you don't teach a dog to yield to pressure during a calm training session, they will fight it during a high-stress exam.

We don't just train in your living room. We simulate the vet environment right here in San Jacinto, practicing the specific triggers that cause the meltdown: the "white coat" presence, the feel of a cold table, and the restraint needed for blood draws or ear cleanings.
